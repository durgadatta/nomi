# J

> Status: comparative evidence; scope is the official vocabulary.

## Observe

J organizes its vocabulary into functions and higher-order operators, with
compact ASCII spellings and distinct one-argument/two-argument meanings for
many primitives. Its uniformity is semantic, not the absence of punctuation.
[Primary reference: vocabulary](https://www.jsoftware.com/help/dictionary/vocabul.htm).

## Judge And Pay

**Adopt** a small compositional operation family. **Reject** context-dependent
operator meanings and tacit composition as global Nomi defaults. **Defer** an
array DSL until named calls expose a concrete insufficiency.

The cost is losing concise algebra that becomes readable with expertise.
Replacing a glyph with an English word does not automatically explain its
shape laws. Nomi should therefore document those laws first rather than make
a superficial argument that verbosity is inherently clear.

## Test

Express a group/reduce/reshape workflow as named calls with intermediate
shapes, then compare a scoped concise representation. Ask reviewers to predict
output shape and failure location. Do not reopen core composition operators
unless the scoped experiment demonstrates a need that ordinary calls cannot
meet. See [numerics](../features/numerics.md) and
[collections](../features/collections.md).
